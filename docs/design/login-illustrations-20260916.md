## satellite-fields-20260916.png

> Use case: stylized-concept
> Asset type: premium agricultural satellite analysis login page hero illustration, portrait 1024x1536.
> Primary request: an elegant stylized 3D editorial illustration of satellite remote sensing over a floating island of agricultural fields.
> Scene/backdrop: seamless very dark forest green backdrop, matte and calm. The upper 30 percent is mostly empty dark forest negative space for web headings that will be added in HTML.
> Subject: in the middle and lower portion, a beautifully composed small floating terrain island made of irregular adjacent farm plots, precise parallel crop rows, a narrow winding irrigation stream, a few rounded trees, warm ivory farm paths, and subtly glowing mint parcel outlines. One tasteful small ivory earth observation satellite with dark blue-green solar panels floats above the fields, with very subtle thin observation lines descending to one parcel.
> Style/medium: refined soft clay 3D with architectural model precision, tactile matte materials, clean rounded forms, no toy-like cartoon faces, nuanced details, gentle isometric view.
> Lighting/mood: soft warm sunlight on crop tips, tranquil and sophisticated, restrained ambient occlusion, subtle green glow.
> Color palette: deep forest and emerald greens, soft sage and mint highlights, muted golden wheat, ivory accents. Background approximately #101d17, clean and homogeneous around outer edges.
> Composition/framing: spacious portrait composition; terrain island completely visible with generous breathing room around it, concentrated in lower two-thirds; satellite to upper right of island; no cropping of main objects; render at high quality.
> Constraints: no words, numbers, labels, logos, watermark, charts, UI, people, realistic satellite photo, excessive sci-fi beams or neon. This is decorative conceptual illustration, not measured data.
